# MBAt Club Datathon 2026 — Project Context

This file summarizes the event planning context for the MBAt Club Datathon 2026, for use as background context in Claude Code.

## Event Overview

- **Name:** MBAt Club Datathon 2026
- **Faculty:** Econometrics and Business Statistics
- **Format:** 2-day, daytime only (no overnight stay)
  - **Day 1:** Monday, 21 September — 8:00 AM–10:00 PM
  - **Day 2:** Tuesday, 22 September — 8:00 AM–5:00 PM
- **Location:** Room 132, Learning and Teaching Building (LTB), Clayton campus
- **Participants:** ~60 students in teams of 3–4 (1st- and 2nd-year students combined), roughly 15–20 teams
- **Submission requirements:** A reproducible report (HTML or PDF, within a set word limit) plus a public GitHub repository
- **Judging criteria:** Technical Rigor & Reproducibility · Analytical Insight · Professional Communication
- **Awards:** Top 3 teams (of 5 finalists who present) receive prizes
- **Catering budget:** ~$2,000 total for ~74 heads (60 participants + 3 judges + 3 staff + ~8 contingency) across both days
  - Day 1: snacks only (cookies, lollies, muffins — none/GF/veg options) + drinks (coffee, soft drink, juice) available all day; no catered lunch/dinner (self-paced, on your own)
  - Day 2: Subway lunch (none/veg/GF options) as part of the 11:30 AM–2:00 PM block, plus drinks/snacks
